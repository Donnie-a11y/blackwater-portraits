Senior portraits in the Ohio Valley: a guide for the Class of 2027

Blackwater Fine Art Portraits · Bellaire, Ohio · August 12, 2026

When to book, what actually matters, and why the yearbook photo is the least important one you will take.

If you have a rising senior, you are about to get a year that moves faster than you expect. Here is what actually matters, from someone who photographs it.

When to book

Late summer through October is the window. The light is better, the fields are still alive, and your senior is not yet buried under applications and a schedule that belongs to someone else. If you wait until spring, you are photographing a kid who is already halfway gone.

The yearbook photo is not the point

Your school will get its headshot. That image will live in a book on a shelf. It is not the picture you will want in ten years.

The one you will want is the one where they look like themselves, the posture they actually have, the half-smile you have been looking at since they were four, the person they were right before they left. That is a portrait, and it belongs on a wall, not in a folder.

A senior portrait made where she feels like herself, not a backdrop.

Bring the things that are theirs

The truck. The jersey. The instrument. The dog. Seventeen years old is a specific person with specific obsessions, and in a decade those objects will matter more than the outfit. Especially the dog.

If you are not sure what will actually photograph well, our what to wear guide walks through it, and we plan the details with every senior before the session so nothing is left to chance.

Guys count too. A strong senior portrait works for every student.

This is mostly for you

Seniors think senior portraits are for them. They are not. They are for the parent who is going to walk past that wall every day after the room goes quiet. I have photographed enough of these to know exactly who cries at the reveal, and it is never the senior.

What a session looks like here

We plan it first, wardrobe, locations, what they care about. We shoot in the studio, around Bellaire, or wherever fits them. This is the same guided, unhurried Portrait Experience we design for every client, start to finish.

Then you come back and we design the artwork at true size, on your wall, so it is finished properly instead of living on a hard drive. You can see how we plan and price finished pieces on our investment page, or explore more senior portraits before you reach out.

Late summer through fall photographs best, but the Ohio Valley is beautiful in every season.

Senior portrait questions

When should seniors take portraits?

Late summer through October is the ideal window in the Ohio Valley. The light is better and your senior is not yet buried under applications.

How long is a senior session?

Sessions are unhurried and fully guided. We plan wardrobe and locations beforehand, then design your finished artwork together about a week later.

Do you photograph seniors in the Ohio Valley?

Yes. Our studio is in Bellaire, Ohio, and we photograph seniors across the Ohio Valley, Belmont County, St. Clairsville, Wheeling, and Northern West Virginia.
