04 · Meet the Artist

Donnie Stevens

I have had a camera in my hands since childhood. Through my company DLS Innovations, my team photographs thousands of students, athletes, and families across the region every year.

Blackwater is where I slow down. This is my fine art studio, where I create commissioned artwork one family, one senior, and one animal at a time. The result is never a folder of files. It is artwork made for your walls and made to be passed down.

I am especially passionate about animal and equine portraiture, and my goal is for Blackwater to become the premier animal portrait studio in the Ohio Valley.
