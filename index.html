04 · Meet the Artist

Donnie Stevens

I have had a camera in my hands since childhood. Through my company DLS Innovations, my team photographs thousands of students and athletes across the region every year.

Blackwater is where I slow down. This is my fine art studio, where I create commissioned artwork one family, one senior, and one animal at a time. The result is never a folder of files. It is artwork made for your walls and made to be passed down.

We are passionate about both the people and the animals you love, and every portrait is created as fine art for your walls.
