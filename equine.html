The Vision

An artist's eye for light, form, and presence

Donnie photographs horse and owner together, and the horse alone, with the patience and reverence the subject deserves. This is not snapshots at the barn. It is fine art, composed for the wall, rendered with the drama and grace a horse carries naturally.

Equine portraiture is a personal passion and a signature ambition of the studio. Each commission is created individually, on location where your horse is most at ease, and finished to the same heirloom standard as every Blackwater piece.
