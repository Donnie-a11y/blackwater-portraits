Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Every family should be able to explore our work and reach us with ease.

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

Blackwater Portraits wants this website to work well for everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, or other assistive technology.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. That includes readable text contrast, clear headings, descriptive image text, forms with labels, visible keyboard focus, and a Skip to main content link at the top of every page.

Report a problem

If anything on the site is difficult to use, or you need information in another format, please tell us. Let us know the page and what happened, and we will work with you to fix it.

Email: info@blackwaterportraits.com
Phone or text: (740) 391-4921

We reply within 2 business days.

Ongoing work

We review the site as we add new pages and photographs. Some content from third parties, such as embedded maps or social media, may not be fully within our control, and we will help you reach that information another way if you need it.
