Animals

The ones with four legs

Blackwater Fine Art Portraits · Bellaire, Ohio

He is in every photo on your phone and not one of them is any good. Let us fix that.

There is a dog asleep in your house right now who has been in roughly four thousand photographs, and you do not love a single one of them.

Because a snapshot cannot see him

A phone flattens an animal. It gets the shape but misses the thing you actually love, the particular set of the ears, the ridiculous dignity, the look he gives you and nobody else. That is what a portrait is for.

Yes, they can sit still. No, it does not matter

The most common thing I hear is he would never sit still for that. He does not have to. Studio lighting freezes motion cold, and honestly the best animal portraits are rarely the sitting ones. They are the ones with something happening behind the eyes.

Our studio dog is a German Shepherd and he has never once done what he was told. He photographs beautifully anyway.

Horses too

Equine portraiture is its own thing, and there is very little of it done properly anywhere near here. If you have a horse that matters to you, we should talk. We will come to the barn.

The part nobody says out loud

You have maybe ten or twelve years with a dog. Most people photograph them properly zero times, and then one day it is too late and all you have is four thousand phone pictures and none of them are him.

That is the whole reason this page exists.

Artwork of the one who follows you room to room

You have maybe ten or twelve years. It is worth doing properly once.
