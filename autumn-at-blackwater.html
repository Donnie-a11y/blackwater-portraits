A Season That Does Not Wait

Eight families. One autumn.

There are about two weeks a year when the Ohio Valley turns gold and the light goes soft in the late afternoon. We photograph eight families in that window, and then it is gone until next year.

What you take home is not a phone snapshot. It is finished, gallery grade wall art, created with the same artistry as any Blackwater commission.
