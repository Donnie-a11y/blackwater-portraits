Meet the Artist

The artist behind the artwork

A portrait artist who has spent his life learning exactly how to make people feel at ease and look like themselves.

Donnie Stevens · Artist & Owner

A camera in hand
since childhood

Photography has been my life's work for as long as I can remember. Through my company DLS Innovations, my team photographs thousands of students, athletes, families, and organizations across the region every single year. That volume taught me something most photographers never get to learn: how to read a person in seconds, direct with confidence, and find the real expression under the nerves.

Blackwater is where I bring that craft to a single family at a time. This is my fine art studio, unhurried and personal, built around one belief. The people you love deserve to become artwork on your walls, not files lost on a phone.

Donnie Stevens
Artist & Owner, Blackwater Fine Art Portraits
