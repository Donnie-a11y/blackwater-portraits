Behind the Studio

A new fine art portrait studio in Bellaire

Blackwater Fine Art Portraits · The Ohio Valley

The Ohio Valley has no shortage of cameras. What it has lacked is a studio that treats your family as fine art, from the first conversation to the finished piece on your wall. Blackwater is here to change that.

Blackwater Fine Art Portraits has opened its doors in Bellaire, Ohio, a fine art portrait studio creating heirloom family, children, senior, and pet portraits for families across the Ohio Valley, Belmont County, Wheeling, and Northern West Virginia. This is not a photo session and a folder of files. It is commissioned artwork, designed for your home and made to be passed down.

Why the Ohio Valley deserves this

For years, families here have driven hours to Pittsburgh or beyond for a true fine art portrait experience. Blackwater brings that level of craft home. Every commission is planned around the wall it will live on, photographed in an unhurried, fully guided session, and finished as gallery grade wall art with archival materials and hand finished framing.

The artist behind the studio

Blackwater is the fine art studio of Donnie Stevens. Through his company DLS Innovations, Donnie and his team photograph thousands of students and athletes across the region every year. That volume taught him something most photographers never learn: how to read a person in seconds, direct with confidence, and find the real expression under the nerves. Donnie is honored to be named a 2026 Professional Photographers of America 30 Under 30 Finalist, and Blackwater is where he slows that craft down to one family at a time.

What we create

Blackwater specializes in four artforms, each held to one standard. Family portraits in the Heirloom Series. Children's portraits in the Legacy Series. High school senior portraits in the Signature Series. And fine art pet portraits in the Companion Series. This holiday season, we also introduce something new: The Wonder of Christmas, a fine art Santa experience unlike anything in the Valley.
