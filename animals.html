What This Is

Ohio Valley pet portraits, created with intention. We treat your dog or pet with the same artistry, patience, and care as any family portrait. Whether it is a soulful solo study or the two of you together, the bond is the subject.

How It Works

Four steps, without pressure

01

Planning

A relaxed conversation about your animal, their temperament, and the wall you want to fill. We choose the setting together, in the Bellaire studio or a place that is familiar to them.

02

The Session

Calm, patient, and led entirely by your animal. There is no rush and no pressure, so their personality has room to show, whether it is a solo study or the two of you together.

03

Design & Reveal

You return to the studio to see your portraits at true scale and design your artwork together, choosing the pieces, sizes, and finishes that belong in your home.

04

Your Artwork

Archival materials, hand finished framing, and a Certificate of Authenticity. You collect your finished pieces in person at the studio, ready for your walls.
