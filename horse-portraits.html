Four steps, on your horse's time

01

Planning

A relaxed conversation about your horse, their routine and the wall you want to fill. We choose the setting together, usually your barn or a field they know.

02

The session

Calm and patient, led by your horse. Horse and owner together, the horse alone, or both.

03

Design and reveal

You return to the studio to see your portraits at true scale and design your artwork, choosing the pieces, sizes and finishes for your home.

04

Your artwork

Archival materials, with larger works mounted on Masonite to last. Hand finished custom framing is available. You collect your pieces at the studio.

Artwork and investment

Build the collection that fits your home

Your collection is built around your home, not chosen from a fixed package. After your session you see your portraits at true scale and choose the pieces, sizes and finishes that belong on your walls.
