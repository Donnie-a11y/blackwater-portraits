A Full Service Studio

The Process

Five steps, fully guided

01

Inquiry

It begins with a message. We learn about your family or your animal, your home, and the walls you want to fill.

02

Consultation

A relaxed planning conversation. We design the session around your story, your wardrobe, and the finished pieces you have in mind.

03

Portrait Session

Unhurried and fully directed. Your only job is to be present while we create with calm, confident guidance.

04

Design & Reveal

You return to the studio to see your portraits at true scale and design your artwork together, nothing left to guesswork.

05

Finished Artwork

Your pieces are crafted to an heirloom standard. You collect your finished artwork at the studio, ready for your walls.

The Whole Point

Artwork for the home,
not files for a phone

Anyone can hand you a folder of images. Blackwater exists to create something you will actually live with. Your portraits are finished as gallery grade wall art, albums, and heirloom pieces, each one designed for a specific place in your home.

  • Every piece designed at true scale for your walls
  • Archival materials built to last for generations
  • A Certificate of Authenticity with each artwork
  • Collected in person at the studio, beautifully packaged
