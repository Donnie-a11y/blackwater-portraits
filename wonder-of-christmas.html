The Experience

Not a line at the mall.
Time with Santa, unhurried.

Our Santa is the real thing. A gentle, storybook Santa, a Christmas set we build by hand, and one family in the studio at a time, so your children have time to talk, laugh, and get to know him instead of being rushed through a line.

The set goes up in pieces every year. The window frame, the tree, the trunk, the rocking horse, the rug. None of it is a backdrop and none of it is generated. Your children can touch all of it.
