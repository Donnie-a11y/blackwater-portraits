Our Santa is the real thing. A gentle, storybook Santa, a beautifully styled set, and a calm, guided session where your children are seen, not rushed. The result is heirloom wall art that holds the magic of this Christmas for a lifetime.

Before we open this experience to the public, we are inviting three families to live it first and help us make it perfect.

Session Expectations

What the day looks like

  • Plan to be with us for about an hour, unhurried and fully directed
  • Sessions take place at the Bellaire studio on our styled Christmas set
  • We guide wardrobe beforehand, warm neutrals and timeless holiday tones
  • Your children set the pace, we are patient with every age and temperament
  • You will return for a guided reveal to choose any artwork for your home

Model Release

Clear, simple, and kind

Because this session is complimentary, selected families sign a straightforward model release before the shoot.

  • You grant Blackwater permission to use your session images and video in marketing, including our website, social media, print, and advertising
  • Your portraits are always created with taste and care, and presented the way any client's artwork would be
  • A parent or legal guardian signs on behalf of any child under eighteen
  • The release is provided in full for your review before you commit, no surprises
