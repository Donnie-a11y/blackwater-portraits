Family

The best time of year for family portraits

Blackwater Fine Art Portraits · Bellaire, Ohio

Everyone says fall. They are right, but not for the reason they think.

Ask ten people in Belmont County when to take family photos and ten will say fall. They are right. But the reason everyone gives is the wrong one.

It is not the leaves

Yes, the Ohio Valley turns. Yes, it is beautiful for about eleven days. But color is not what makes a fall portrait work, the light does. Late September through October, the sun sits lower and softer all day, and it does something to skin that no amount of editing replicates.

The real answer: the season you are in right now

Here is the thing nobody tells you. The best time is the season you are in right now.

Every family I photograph says a version of the same sentence: we have been meaning to do this for years. And in those years, a kid stopped being little. Someone's hair went grey. A grandparent got slower. The portrait you keep postponing is a portrait of a family that will not exist in that form again.

Fall light is better. But no light is worth two years.

Practically speaking

Fall books out first, and it books early, most of it is gone by early September. Winter light in the studio is gorgeous and almost nobody uses it. Spring is green and forgiving. Summer evenings in the Valley are underrated.

There is no bad season. There is only the difference between having it and meaning to. See family portraits or the experience.
