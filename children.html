What This Is

A real smile, never a forced one

These are not rushed sessions. Donnie has photographed thousands of children and knows how to earn a true expression, the kind you recognize instantly as your own child. The result is honest, artistic children's portrait artwork, finished as pieces that belong in your home.

Every session is planned around your child, not a template. We talk through their personality, the things they love, and the room the artwork will live in, so the finished portraits feel like them and still look right on your walls for years to come.

How It Works

Four steps, at their pace

01

Planning

A relaxed conversation about your child, your home, and the wall you want to fill. We choose the setting together, in the Bellaire studio or on location, and plan clothing they will feel comfortable in.

02

The Session

Patient, playful, and unhurried. Your child sets the pace while Donnie guides gently, waiting for the real expression you recognize as theirs. Siblings can be photographed together and on their own.

03

Design & Reveal

You return to the studio to see your portraits at true scale and design your artwork together, choosing the pieces, sizes, and finishes that belong in your home.

04

Your Artwork

Archival materials, hand finished framing, and a Certificate of Authenticity. You collect your finished pieces in person at the studio, ready for your walls.
