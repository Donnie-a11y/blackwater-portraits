The Experience

Where should your portrait hang?

Blackwater Fine Art Portraits · Bellaire, Ohio

A room-by-room guide to the walls in your home, and what belongs on each one.

Most people order a portrait and then figure out where it goes. Backward. The wall should decide the artwork, not the other way around.

Above the sofa

The most common wall in any home, and the most commonly under-filled. The rule designers use: your artwork should span roughly two thirds the width of the furniture beneath it. For a standard sofa, that is a single large piece or a grouping, almost never the modest one people instinctively reach for.

The entry

This is the wall that tells everyone who walks in what this family is. It is usually narrow, which makes it perfect for one strong vertical portrait at eye level. It is the highest impact wall in most homes and the most neglected.

The hallway

The best hallways in the world are galleries. A run of matched pieces, the kids individually, the family together, the dog, turns dead space into the part of the house people slow down in.

Over the bed

Quieter. Softer. This is where the portrait of the two of you goes, the one that predates the children and reminds you the family started as a couple.

The stairwell

Hard to shoot, hard to design, worth it. A vertical grouping climbing the wall is the closest thing a home has to a spine.

The rule under all of it

Bigger than you think. Higher than feels right is usually wrong, center at eye level, around 57 inches. And this is exactly why we design at true size on your actual wall, so none of the above is a guess. See the experience or the investment.
