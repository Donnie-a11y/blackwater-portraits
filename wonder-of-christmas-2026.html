Wonder of Christmas

The Wonder of Christmas is coming

Blackwater Fine Art Portraits · Bellaire, Ohio

Our Santa experience returns in October. Ten sessions, two days, and then it is gone.

You know the version of this everyone has done. A folding line at the mall, a stranger in a rented suit, ninety seconds, a crying toddler, and a print you throw away in March.

This is not that.

What we built

The Wonder of Christmas is a hand-built set in our Bellaire studio. A real Santa, the kind who talks to your child like a person and never rushes. Time to warm up. Time to be shy first. Nobody in line behind you.

And at the end, artwork. Finished, framed, made to come out of a box every December for the next thirty years and mean more each time.

Why we are telling you in September

Because it happens in October. Christmas artwork has to be photographed, designed, printed and finished long before December, and every studio that tells you otherwise in November is selling you a rush job.

The families who do this well plan it now.

Ten sessions, two days

We are photographing only ten families, on Saturday, October 24 and Sunday, October 25. That is the whole season. Once the ten times are filled, they are filled, and we do not add more, because the entire point is that nobody is rushed.

One honest note

Your child will be small for a very short window. The Santa photo is the one your family will pull out every single year without fail, it is the most reliably revisited portrait we make. It is worth doing properly once. Reserve your family's date.
