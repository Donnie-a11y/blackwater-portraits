How It Works

Four steps, fully guided

01

Planning

A relaxed conversation about your family, your home, and the wall you want to fill. We choose the setting together, in the Bellaire studio or on location, and plan what everyone will wear.

02

The Session

Unhurried and fully directed. Your only job is to be present while Donnie guides everyone, from the youngest to the grandparents, with calm, confident direction.

03

Design & Reveal

You return to the studio to see your portraits at true scale and design your artwork together, choosing the pieces, sizes, and finishes that belong in your home.

04

Your Artwork

Archival materials, hand finished framing, and a Certificate of Authenticity. You collect your finished pieces in person at the studio, ready for your walls.

Family portrait questions

In the Bellaire, Ohio studio or on location in a setting that suits your story, from the family farm to the Ohio Valley outdoors. We plan the setting together during your consultation.

We guide you through it. During planning we help you choose clothing that is timeless and cohesive, in soft neutrals and textures that keep the focus on your family. Our What to Wear guide is a good place to start.

Every season has its own light. Photographing in fall means your artwork is on the wall before the holidays, and our seasonal guide walks through each one.

From inquiry to finished artwork, most commissions span a few weeks. That includes your consultation, session, design and reveal appointment, and the time to craft your pieces to an heirloom standard.

Blackwater is a wall art studio first. Digital files accompany the finished artwork you commission rather than being sold on their own, and your complete digital collection is included once your collection reaches $5,000.
