The Experience

Why we design your portraits at true size

Blackwater Fine Art Portraits · Bellaire, Ohio

Nobody can picture a 30x40 from a thumbnail. So we stopped asking people to try.

Here is a thing that happens in this industry constantly. A family is handed a price list with sizes on it. They pick a number that sounds reasonable. The portrait arrives, they hang it, and it looks small. Not wrong, just smaller than the moment deserved. And nobody wants to spend it twice.

Inches on a page mean nothing

Say sixteen by twenty out loud and try to see it. You cannot. Neither can I, and I do this for a living. A number on a price sheet is not information, it is a guess dressed up as a decision.

So we show you instead

After your session, you come back to the studio and we design your artwork together. Your images go up at true size, against a wall the same size as yours, so you see exactly what a piece will look like at home before anything is ordered. Not a thumbnail. Not a mockup. The actual footprint.

It takes about an hour, and it is most people's favorite part of the whole thing, because that is the moment your family stops being a file and becomes an object with weight.

What tends to happen

People go bigger than they planned. Every time. Not because anyone pushed, but because once you see it at true size, the small one is obviously wrong. The wall tells you. We just stop it from being a guess.

There is no hard sell in that room

You are not cornered, there is no countdown, and there is no script. If the right answer for your home is one piece, the right answer is one piece. My job is to make sure you never hang something you regret. You can see the full process on the experience page, and what it costs on the investment page.
