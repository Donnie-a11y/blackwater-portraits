Heirloom Artwork

Designed for the room it lives in

By Donnie Stevens · Bellaire, Ohio · August 6, 2026

A portrait is not finished when the session ends. It is finished when it is on your wall, at the right size, in the right place, holding the room the way only real artwork can.

We design for the room, not just the wall

Before a single image is chosen, we talk about where it will live. The wall above the sofa. The space over the sideboard. The height of the ceiling, the light from the window, the warmth of the wood and the stone. Your portrait is planned for that room, at the size it deserves, so it looks intentional the day it goes up. The collection above was designed as a set, three moments arranged to read as one.

Seen at true scale, at your Design Appointment

At your Design Appointment we show your images on your actual wall, at true scale, so nothing is left to guesswork. You see a piece at thirty inches and again at fifty, and you feel the difference in the room. Most families are surprised how much larger the right piece wants to be, and how much a space asks for it once they can see it.

One statement piece, or a collection

Some walls want a single large piece that anchors the whole room. Others come alive as a grouping, a few moments arranged with intention so they read as one. We design both, and we design them to belong together. You can see how the full process unfolds, from first hello to the finished piece, on the experience page.

A single statement piece, sized to hold the room on its own.

Made to be lived with

Gallery grade materials, hand finished framing, and a Certificate of Authenticity. Wall Art built to hang for decades and to be handed down, not filed away on a drive. You can explore what it looks like to commission a piece for your home on our investment page, or meet the artist behind the studio on our about page.

FAQ

How large should a wall portrait be?

We design each piece for its specific wall at true scale during your Design Appointment, so it fits the room the first time.

Can I display a collection instead of one piece?

Yes. We design single statement pieces and coordinated groupings, both created to belong together.

Where are you located?

Our studio is in Bellaire, Ohio, creating for families across the Ohio Valley.
